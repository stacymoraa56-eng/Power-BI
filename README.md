# Business Analytics Portfolio

Welcome to my Business analytics portfolio! This repository showcases projects demonstrating my ability to clean and analyze data, develop dashboards, identify business trends, and communicate actionable insights.

The projects explore different business contexts, from company sales performance to hospital operations and pharmacy expenditure. Each project includes its own documentation and supporting deliverables.

## Projects

### 1. Company Sales Analysis

**Objective:** Analyze company sales data to understand business performance, identify sales trends, and uncover opportunities for improvement.

This project demonstrates the use of data analysis and visualization to transform sales data into meaningful business insights.

**Key focus areas**

* Sales performance and trends.
* Comparison of business performance across relevant categories.
* Identification of patterns that can support business decisions.
* Presentation of findings through data visualizations.

### 2. Hospital & Pharmacy Data Analysis

**Objective:** Analyze hospital patient visits, diagnoses, medication consumption, pharmacy expenditure, and length of hospital stay to identify operational and financial patterns.

This project investigates how disease patterns vary across counties, how patient volumes relate to pharmacy revenue, which departments incur the highest pharmacy costs, and how medication consumption and spending differ across age groups and diagnoses.

**Key focus areas**

* Disease patterns across counties.
* Patient visits compared with pharmacy revenue.
* Pharmacy costs across hospital departments.
* Medication consumption across age groups.
* Pharmacy spending and length of stay by diagnosis.

**Tools:** Power BI and any additional tools actually used in the project.

**Explore the project:** [Hospital & Pharmacy Data Analysis](./Hospital-Pharmacy-Analysis/README.md)
